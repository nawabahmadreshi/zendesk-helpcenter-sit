Overview

The Aquera platform enables your organization to scale complex data management by automating data integration across various resource types. Aquera's integrations facilitate automatic provisioning, ensuring consistency across systems. It enables organizations to streamline processes, dynamically scale resources, and automate repetitive tasks, ultimately enhancing efficiency in managing complex IT infrastructures and applications.

This integration automates identity-driven IT service management workflows by connecting your HR system with Jira Service Management (JSM). The integration retrieves workforce data from the selected HR application, detects employee lifecycle events based on the configured lifecycle rules, and automatically creates Jira Service Management tickets for the configured lifecycle events.

The integration supports configurable ticket creation for employee lifecycle events, such as new hires and terminations. It also allows you to control when tickets are created, map employee information to Jira ticket fields, and customize how employees are matched between the HR system and Jira Service Management.
